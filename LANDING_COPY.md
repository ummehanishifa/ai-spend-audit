## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card. No account needed.
Enter your tools, get your audit, done. We only ask for
your email if you want a copy of the report sent to you.

**Q: How does it know if I'm overpaying?**
A: We maintain a database of current pricing for every
major AI tool. The audit engine checks your plan and
seat count against the actual pricing tiers and flags
cases where a cheaper plan would cover your usage.
Every recommendation has a one-line reason attached.

**Q: Will you sell my data?**
A: No. We store your audit results to generate your
shareable link and send you the report. We do not sell
or share your data with third parties. Your email is
only used to send your report and relevant Credex offers.

**Q: What AI tools does it support?**
A: Currently supports Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude,
ChatGPT, Gemini, and Windsurf. We update pricing weekly
and add new tools based on user requests.

**Q: What is Credex and why are they mentioned?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI credits — the same tools
you already use, at lower prices. They built this audit
tool as a free resource. If your audit shows significant
savings potential, Credex may reach out to show you how
to save even more through discounted credits. There is
no obligation.
