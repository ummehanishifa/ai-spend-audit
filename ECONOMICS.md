# Economics

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits — typically 20-40% below
retail price. A startup spending $500/month on AI tools
could save $100-200/month by buying through Credex.

**Assumptions:**
- Average startup AI spend: $500/month
- Credex discount: 25% below retail
- Credex margin: 10% of transaction value
- Average contract length: 12 months

**Value per converted customer:**
- Monthly AI spend: $500
- Credex takes 10% margin: $50/month
- Annual value per customer: $50 x 12 = $600

**Conservative estimate: $600 LTV per converted lead**

For high-spend customers ($2000+/month):
- Credex margin: $200/month
- Annual value: $2,400 LTV

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel

| Channel | Effort | Est. Users/Week | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reddit posts | 2 hrs/week | 20-40 users | ~$0 |
| Twitter thread | 1 hr/post | 10-30 users | ~$0 |
| IndieHackers | 1 hr/post | 5-15 users | ~$0 |
| Direct outreach | 3 hrs/week | 5-10 users | ~$0 |
| Newsletter mention | 2 hrs outreach | 50-200 users | ~$0 |

**All channels are $0 paid CAC** because this is a
free tool with organic distribution.

The real cost is time:
- Estimated 10 hrs/week for distribution
- At $50/hr opportunity cost: $500/week
- If 100 users/week, CAC = $5/user

## Conversion Funnel

```
1000 visitors
↓ 40% complete audit
400 audit completions
↓ 25% submit email
100 email captures
↓ 10% book consultation
10 Credex consultations
↓ 30% convert to purchase
3 paying customers
```

**Revenue from 1000 visitors:**
- 3 customers x $600 LTV = $1,800
- Revenue per visitor: $1.80

**What needs to be true:**
- Audit completion rate: 40% (reasonable for a free tool)
- Email capture rate: 25% (after value is shown)
- Consultation booking: 10% (for high-savings cases)
- Purchase conversion: 30% (from consultation)

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**Target: $1,000,000 ARR**
**Credex margin per customer: $600/year (conservative)**
**Customers needed: 1,667**

### Month by Month Math

**Months 1-3 (Launch phase):**
- 500 audits/month
- 5 new customers/month
- Revenue: $3,000/month

**Months 4-9 (Growth phase):**
- 2,000 audits/month (Reddit + Twitter compound)
- 20 new customers/month
- Revenue: $12,000/month

**Months 10-18 (Scale phase):**
- 5,000 audits/month
- 50 new customers/month
- Cumulative customers: ~600
- Revenue: $30,000/month = $360,000 ARR

**To hit $1M ARR:**
- Need 1,667 active customers
- At 50 new customers/month: 33 months
- To hit it in 18 months: need 93 customers/month
- Requires 9,300 audits/month at 1% conversion

**What has to be true:**
1. The tool goes viral at least once
   (a tweet or Reddit post gets 500+ upvotes)
2. Credex sales team converts consultations at 40%+
   instead of 30%
3. Average customer LTV increases to $1,200
   (higher-spend customers or upsells)
4. The tool expands beyond individual audits to
   team dashboards — increasing stickiness and
   repeat visits

## Summary

The unit economics are strong:
- CAC is near zero with organic distribution
- LTV of $600-2,400 per customer
- The free tool pays for itself with 1-2 conversions/month

The challenge is volume — getting enough audits
completed to generate enough leads for Credex's
sales team to work with.

The viral loop (shareable URL) is the key lever.
Every shared audit is a free ad that drives
more audits which drives more leads.
